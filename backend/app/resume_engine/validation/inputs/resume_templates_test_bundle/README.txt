# Resume Template Test Bundle (Realistic Dummy Data)

This archive contains 5 distinct styles and formats of resumes built for testing resume parsers, ATS filters, document indexers, and layout analysis software.

## Included Resume Archetypes:

1. **01_Modern_Tech_Software_Engineer.docx**
   - **Target Persona:** Alex Chen, Senior Full Stack Software Engineer (7+ years experience)
   - **Structure:** Modern header with live links, concise summary, grouped technical competencies, reverse-chronological experience with metric-driven accomplishments, education and AWS/CKA certifications.
   - **ATS Attributes:** High density of tech keywords, cloud infrastructure terms, measurable outcomes.

2. **02_Executive_Corporate_Director.docx**
   - **Target Persona:** Eleanor R. Vance, Vice President of Global Operations
   - **Structure:** Classic executive layout, executive profile, 3x3 core competency matrix (table layout), strategic leadership positions with P&L accountability, Boardroom governance, MBA and Six Sigma credentials.
   - **ATS Attributes:** Executive title taxonomy, multi-million dollar budget metrics, governance keywords.

3. **03_Creative_Product_UX_Designer.docx**
   - **Target Persona:** Marcus Brody, Lead Product & UX Designer
   - **Structure:** Contemporary design layout with teal accent palette, design philosophy, Figma/research toolkits, product launch metrics, case study milestones.
   - **ATS Attributes:** Human-centered design keywords, accessibility (WCAG), conversion metrics.

4. **04_Academic_Research_CV.docx**
   - **Target Persona:** Dr. Sophia Al-Mansoor, Ph.D. in Computational Biology
   - **Structure:** Comprehensive Academic CV format, multi-page layout style, peer-reviewed publications (APA format), doctoral dissertation details, NIH grants, and academic appointments.
   - **ATS Attributes:** Publication citation parsing, academic degree hierarchy, grant funding structures.

5. **05_Entry_Level_Business_Analyst.docx**
   - **Target Persona:** Jordan Taylor, Junior Business Analyst
   - **Structure:** Education-first layout, GPA/honors, coursework, analytical tools (SQL, Tableau, Excel), corporate internships, and university capstone project.
   - **ATS Attributes:** Early career parsing, education extraction, internship distinguishing.
