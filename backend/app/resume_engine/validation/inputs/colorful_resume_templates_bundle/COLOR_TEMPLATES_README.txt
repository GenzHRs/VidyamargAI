# Colorful Resume Templates Suite (PDF + DOCX)

This bundle contains vibrant, styled resume templates created specifically to test how parsing systems, OCR engines, and ATS filters process modern color palettes, tinted blocks, and visual accents.

## 🎨 Included Colorful Resume Templates:

1. **`01_DOCX_Vibrant_Coral_Creative_Resume.docx`**
   - **Color Theme:** Coral Header (`#FF5A5F`), Soft Rose Tint (`#FFF1F2`), Slate text.
   - **Archetype:** Maya Lin-Vazquez (Senior Brand Designer & Art Director).
   - **Format:** Top horizontal banner with 2-column peach sidebar.

2. **`02_DOCX_Navy_Teal_Cloud_Architect.docx`**
   - **Color Theme:** Deep Navy Sidebar (`#0F172A`), Vibrant Teal Accents (`#2DD4BF`), Crisp White Body.
   - **Archetype:** Darius K. Vance (Principal Cloud Architect).
   - **Format:** Full-height dark navy sidebar paired with multi-tiered tech sections.

3. **`03_PDF_Royal_Purple_Marketing_Resume.pdf`**
   - **Color Theme:** Royal Purple Header (`#6B21A8`), Soft Lavender Background (`#FAF5FF`).
   - **Archetype:** Camila Restrepo (Senior Growth Marketing Manager).
   - **Format:** High-contrast vector PDF with marketing metrics and ad-budget taxonomy.

4. **`04_PDF_Emerald_Forest_Operations_Resume.pdf`**
   - **Color Theme:** Deep Emerald Forest (`#064E3B`), Gold Subheaders (`#FDE047`), Mint Accent Grid (`#D1FAE5`).
   - **Archetype:** Julian A. Mercer (Director of Business Operations).
   - **Format:** Structured 3-column competency matrix table and executive timeline.

---
## 🔍 Parser Test Objectives for Colorful Resumes:
- **Contrast Handling:** Verifies OCR accuracy when reading light text on dark coral/navy/purple backgrounds.
- **Background Filtering:** Confirms that parser does not interpret table cell background colors or border tints as corrupted text or noise.
- **Visual Hierarchy:** Tests whether section headings rendered in custom color HEX codes (Teal, Purple, Rose) are correctly identified as section headers rather than body paragraphs.
